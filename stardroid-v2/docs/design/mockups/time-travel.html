What the phone is actually simulating

So you can trust what you're seeing, and know what it fakes.

  • Real: sidereal rotation at your latitude, correct rise/set geometry, the Sun's annual ecliptic motion, twilight bands computed from true solar altitude, the Moon's ~29.5-day phase cycle and its eastward drift, and the alt-az → screen projection.
  • Faked: planet positions use circular coplanar orbits (so retrograde is qualitatively right but not ephemeris-accurate), and the star field is a scattering with a handful of real bright stars placed approximately.

Everything the concepts depend on — that ground-anchoring holds the horizon still, that sky-anchoring holds stars still, that object-anchoring isolates relative motion — is geometrically honest here.
