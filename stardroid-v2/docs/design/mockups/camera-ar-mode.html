Bench controls

Q1Compositing model

This mock is the chosen model: two independent planes, source-over blend, scrim in between. Camera2-grade exposure control, if needed, comes via CameraX Camera2Interop — it doesn’t force the GL-external-texture model.

Decided: stacked surfaces

Q2Where the correction lives

Fresh DataStore keys for both axes (sensorAzimuthAdjustmentDeg / sensorAltitudeAdjustmentDeg); the old manualCompassAdjustmentDeg key and its Settings row are removed — everyone starts from zero, and dragging here is the only setter. No camera (or permission denied) means no correction feature.

Decided: fresh keys, drag-only Try: open the ⇄ chip

Q3Direct drag vs armed align mode

Dragging adjusts alignment immediately (drag is otherwise unused in Auto mode), with a clear receipt that a correction was applied — snackbar with undo — and an easy reset.

Decided: direct + receipt + reset Try: drag the sky

Q4Night mode × camera

The modes coexist: night mode reds the map and chrome as today; the camera image is not reddened, and night mode imposes a ~65% scrim floor (simulated here) so the video doesn’t wreck dark adaptation — dim further, never brighter.

Decided: coexist + scrim floor Try: night toggle

Q5Does camera-on persist?

Never — the camera layer starts off every launch and the camera is released when the app backgrounds or closes. Session state, not a persisted setting.

Decided: always starts off

Q6Correction visibility

No persistent chip. The correction surfaces in Diagnostics and in a HUD shown only while the on-screen controls are visible — designed separately, alongside FOV and current RA/Dec. The ⇄ chip in this mock stands in for that HUD.

Decided: diagnostics + controls HUD
