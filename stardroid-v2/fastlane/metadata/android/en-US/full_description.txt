Sky Map helps you identify stars, planets, nebulae, and constellations in real-time.

Originally developed as Google Sky Map, it is now open source.

<h3>App permissions:</h3>
Sky Map has <b>no required permissions</b>. Each permission below is optional — you may refuse any of them and still use the app. Nothing is requested at startup; each is asked for only when you first use the feature that needs it, and each can be withdrawn separately in your device settings.
⭐ <b>Location (optional)</b> — used to draw the sky for where you are. Refuse it and you can enter a place name or coordinates by hand instead. Your location never leaves your device.
⭐ <b>Camera (optional)</b> — used only by through-camera mode, which overlays the sky on the live camera view. Refuse it and every other mode works as normal. The image is never recorded or transmitted.
⭐ <b>Notifications (optional)</b> — used only to tell you about meteor-shower peaks and tonight's sky, if you ask for them. They stay off until you turn them on.
