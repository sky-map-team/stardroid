• Notifications: optional alerts for meteor shower peaks, lunar eclipses and a summary of tonight's sky, at most one a day. Everything is off until you turn it on in Settings.
• Widgets: add the Moon, Tonight and Countdown widgets to your Home Screen or Lock Screen for an at-a-glance look at the sky.
• Tonight sheet: a new Tonight sheet gathers what is worth seeing right now - the Moon, visible planets, and any meteor shower or satellite pass coming up.
• New objects: search now finds info cards for Voyager 1, Voyager 2 and BepiColombo.
• iPad: Sky Map now fills larger screens and works in every orientation.
• Satellites: see the International Space Station and other satellites crossing the sky. Satellites are being rolled out gradually to protect the server, so you may not see them yet.
